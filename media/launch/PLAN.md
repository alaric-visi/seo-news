# SEO News launch film: director's plan

**Story.** SEO people start every day opening 19 tabs to find out what Google changed overnight.
SEO News folds them into one calendar: tap a day, see Google's own announcement first, the coverage
grouped under it, the rollout progress and a short brief. Payoff: *every day in SEO, on one calendar.*

**Format.** 1920×1080, 60 fps, 24 s (12 bars at 120 BPM), loop-safe (last frame = first frame:
the logo tile alone, centred). Studio palette from the site: #09090b / #121215, teal #2dd4bf,
mint #5eead4, Inter + DM Mono. No glows on chrome, no particles, no bouncy easing.

**One shape.** A single element, the logo tile, morphs through every state:
tile → search pill → tab bar → tile → calendar → day cell → story card → rollout panel →
brief card → topic row → mini calendar in an archive wall → tile.

**Soundtrack.** Original score written for the film (DECISION: no licensed track needed, beat grid
exact by construction). D minor, 120 BPM. Bars 1–2 filtered intro (pad, hats, pluck);
bar 3 build (snare 8ths → 16ths → 32nds, noise riser, filter opens, 1/8 gap); **drop at 6.0 s**
on the calendar reveal; bars 4–9 full groove (kick, clap, offbeat bass, chord stabs, arp);
bars 10–11 lift; final impact at 22.0 s on the wordmark, tail into the loop.
